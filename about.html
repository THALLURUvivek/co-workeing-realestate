OUR STORY

Creating Innovative Workspaces

At WorkNest, our mission is to transform the way people work by building thoughtfully designed spaces that inspire creativity, foster collaboration, and drive productivity.

Every detail of our spaces is crafted with care — from ergonomic furniture and high-speed internet to collaborative zones and private meeting rooms. We provide everything you need so you can focus on what matters most.

Milestones That Define Us

2019

Founded in Bangalore

2021

Opened locations in Mumbai, Delhi, Hyderabad, and Pune. Crossed 2,000 members across all locations.

50+ Cities & Growing
