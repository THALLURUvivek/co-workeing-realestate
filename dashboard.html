Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a workspace?
To book a workspace, navigate to the Properties section, browse available spaces, and click "Book Now" on your preferred option. Select your desired dates, complete the payment, and you'll receive a confirmation email with all the details.
Can I cancel or modify my booking?
Yes, you can cancel or modify your booking up to 48 hours before the start date for a full refund. Within 48 hours, a 50% cancellation fee applies. To modify, go to My Bookings and click the edit icon next to the booking you want to change.
What payment methods are accepted?
We accept UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), debit cards, credit cards, net banking, and corporate invoicing. All transactions are secured with 256-bit SSL encryption for your safety.
How do I schedule a tour?
You can schedule a free tour from the "Book a Tour" section in your dashboard. Select the property you're interested in, pick a convenient date and time, and our team will confirm your visit within 2 hours. Tours are completely free and non-obligatory.
Is there a free trial available?
Yes! We offer a 1-day free trial for new users. You can access any hot desk space in any city for one full day. Sign up, complete your profile, and claim your free trial from the dashboard. No credit card required.
